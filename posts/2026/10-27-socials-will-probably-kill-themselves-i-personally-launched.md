Socials will probably kill themselves.

I personally launched 50-60 video creatives with AI persona replacement, completely generated by AI in a multi-step process: first the scenario, then the avatar, then the equipment in the video, then each frame, and combining it all together.

Initially they did much worse than my other creatives. After multiple iterations and idea and scenario calibration, I could make them close to the others.

Today I ran into a thing called Pamba, which is a farm of iPhones on US SIM cards. You can create Instagram and TikTok accounts, browse viral content, and have it adapted for your product, plus avatar variations of the same video.

I'm still not sure whether my feelings are positive or negative, because the pipeline is so smart.

That's such a huge bloat in socials, meaning content becomes less and less attractive.

At some point, Pamba might just eat itself due to the huge amount of AI-assisted or AI-created videos.

People won't believe what they see in socials. It will just stop being a good way to market a product.
