I was addicted to games for 5 years.

Now I have 30 minutes max in all socials throughout the day. It used to be 2-3 hours.

I spent every free minute after work playing. I thought it was my relaxation, but it was not. It was an endless loop of quick dopamine doses followed by work. At Toptal we were even playing Counter-Strike together after work on Fridays.

What changed: I had a daughter and I don't want her to feel alone.

I just deleted everything and stopped. I either go full house or don't go at all.

Then at some point socials became kind of a smaller replacement for games. I always had my phone nearby. I started hating it. My girl started copying me scrolling a feed.

This time I tried a softer stop. I made an app that blocks app usage, but with an alternative: useful games for kids, 5-8-10 mins. Every time I open Instagram, I am offered a game to develop my girl.

It worked better than I expected. In the 10 days I've been using it: 34 games, more than an hour of educational games together.

She's always asking me to play Fish or Shark, or Cat and Mouse. That's the biggest impact I've ever got from a thing built solo tbh.
