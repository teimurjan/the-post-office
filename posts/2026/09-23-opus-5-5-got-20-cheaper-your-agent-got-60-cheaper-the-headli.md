Opus 5.5 got 20% cheaper. Your agent got 60% cheaper.

The headline price went from $5/$25 to $4/$20 per million tokens. The cut that matters sits lower on the pricing page. Cache reads dropped 60%, to $0.20 per million. Simon Willison puts it at 90%+ of input tokens billing at the cached rate in a long agent session. GPT-6 Sol shipped an hour later at the same $0.20.

So measure your cache hit rate before you compare models. If you never looked at it, you don't know what either one costs you.

Why cut prices now? Anthropic already made a step towards a slower AI race. I see not only the fear around AI in there. I see the wish to make models profitable. For huge LLMs, optimizing the models is the only way to get there. Otherwise every token is sold on credit.

That's why I want to believe a cheaper Opus means they're working hard to make Opus-like models cheap, not just subsidizing usage to farm training data for RL.
