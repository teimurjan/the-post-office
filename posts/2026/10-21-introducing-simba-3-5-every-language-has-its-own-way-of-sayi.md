Introducing Simba 3.5. Every language has its own way of saying "I love you."

In Seoul, it often sounds like "Have you eaten?"
In Moscow, it's a mother at the door: "Put your hat on, it's minus twenty out."
In Tbilisi, it's a toast to the guests.
In Paris, it's a baker handing over bread that's still warm.

These words hide in small gestures and everyday lines, and they only touch your heart when they sound like home.

That's what we built Simba 3.5 for: one text-to-speech model that speaks 48 languages like a local, out today.

Every voice in this film is Simba 3.5, speaking its own language.

For developers: one model id, simba-3.5, live today on every plan at half price until 21 November.

What's the line that means "I love you" in your language?
