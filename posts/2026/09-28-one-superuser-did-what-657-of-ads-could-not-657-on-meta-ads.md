One superuser did what $657 of ads could not.

$657 on Meta ads for Wait, Professor. 46k impressions, five sign-ups, zero purchases. I fixed the page, narrowed the audience, rebuilt the funnel. To that audience the idea itself looked like AI slop. You cannot funnel your way out of that, so I stopped the ads.

Meanwhile one person started using my other app every day. Catches bugs, tells me what to add and what to cut. 5 versions and 35 builds later, it is shaped around their problem instead of my wide idea.

Then I put ads behind that one. Three campaigns, $602. The first two: 192 clicks, nothing. The third, started on 24 September, has the first two purchases. Both picked the lifetime plan, $83 of ads each.

Not profit yet, but it pays for the next round of ads. Where did your first purchase come from, an audience or one person?
