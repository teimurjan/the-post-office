Yesterday I made my fourth iOS app.

Building iOS apps with agents suits 2026 workflows so well. I've literally automated each step, so from an idea to an MVP I spend 3-4 days.

The last one I bootstrapped in a day. 3 days of polishing and it's already waiting for App Store review.

First, market analysis with Gemini (because it's not blocked where Anthropic and OpenAI are). Then, keyword research with AppTweak MCPs. Scaffold the full app with funnels, analytics, payments, landing, fastlane, localizations. That's it, you already have a solid start.

The next step is where I can't boast the same quick wins. Marketing does not have a universal recipe for any app and it's so easy to end up with slop. The deeper I dive, the less work I delegate to AI here.

Ad campaigns I ran on Meta had 0 results at first. I added more human. Got a couple of purchases, then a pause again. Added even more human. One more lifetime purchase, then silence.

I changed my approach to human-in-the-loop on every step of the creative: strategy, message, visual, format. Results are not clear yet, but I'll share them asap.
