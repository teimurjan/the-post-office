The dashboards will eat your evening before the code does.

Updating App Store metadata by hand used to cost me a full day. I ship in 17 languages, so one copy change meant 17 passes through App Store Connect. Now it is three commands. bun appstore translate, two minutes, then screenshots, then upload. The screenshots come out of a harness I put in the Xcode project, so changing the UI regenerates all of them.

Supabase, Loops, Meta ads, Amplitude. Same idea, every one of them wired into the terminal over MCP, none of them opened in a browser.

Which means I do not actually know those platforms. I need to move fast to test the hypothesis, so I consciously risk quality and deep knowledge.

Where do you draw that line?
