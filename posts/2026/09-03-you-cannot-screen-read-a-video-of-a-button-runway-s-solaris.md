You cannot screen-read a video of a button.

Runway's Solaris renders the interface itself at 720p, frame by frame. A language model decides what changes, a world model paints it. No DOM, no source, nothing to diff in a PR.

I've been around this topic all year. I'm so happy with programmatic video creation using Remotion for ProductHunt demos and info-heavy video creatives. Watching a video become the interface is crazy, and it is surely the next step in UI engineering.

It'd be madness. Buying a sofa by walking a camera through the shop with arrows, dragging the one you want to checkout, paying with your card. Now imagine that with a screen reader. Runway admits stable text is still hard and screen readers are not there.

I am one of the excited ones. So that part is on us.
