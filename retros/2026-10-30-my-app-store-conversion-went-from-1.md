## The cause, and the decision

The craft was fine and the room was small. This post has the traits the recent retros asked
for: the owner's words, a turn, a measured result, a real artifact, a stance at the end. It
still landed in the bottom five of the lane. The subject is the difference from the 10-13
breakout: one unnamed app's UI and a pricing slider, against shipping apps with agents, which
many builders have tried. One post cannot separate that from the early scrape or from the
network split, which is not on record.

**Decision: modify.** Keep the emails and the turn. Change the subject's size: tell the next
version from the problem many builders share (users who cancel and only say they don't like
it), with the slider as the example instead of the main object. Put a number on retention
when one exists.
