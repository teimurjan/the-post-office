## The cause, and the decision

The craft was fine and the room was small. This is not the news-without-firsthand
miss: the post carried a real firsthand line ("I keep a repo of my own skills…
the service part I hand to MCPs now"), and it still landed at t0's center. That
is a second negative data point for [[audience]]'s open question — a
**process-level** firsthand line did not promote a t0 study subject to t1,
consistent with `firsthand_promotion: unevidenced`.

**Decision: modify.** Keep the hook discipline and keep the firsthand layer —
both were right. Change the subject selection: a single study, benchmark, or
paper is a t0 room, and a firsthand *take* about one's own workflow does not
buy it out of that band. Draft a lone-study subject only when a runnable
firsthand artifact could plausibly promote it (still unproven), or accept it as
a t0 floor post and price the reach accordingly. Do not read this post's clean
craft as a reason to repeat single-paper subjects for reach.
