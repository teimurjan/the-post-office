## The cause, and the decision

The craft was fine and the room was small, and this time the room was small on
purpose. The score said 0, the post landed in 0's band, and the layer bought at
most the top of it. The cost of the test was one publishing slot, and the answer
is worth more than that.

**Decision: modify.** The wedge survives — "most of your calls are if-statements"
was confirmed by a practitioner in the comments — and the subject is what to
change. The room for that argument is the reader's own pipeline, the frontier
prices they pay for yes/no and pick-one calls, which is a thing they have fought
with. Jev and Kev are the instance, not the subject. Lead with the cost the reader
already pays and the vendor becomes evidence.

And stop overriding a t0 score on the strength of a firsthand story. The only
firsthand form with an open claim to promotion is a runnable artifact, and this
post did not carry one.
