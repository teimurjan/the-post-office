first scrape about 190 hours (7.9 days) after publishing. Likes (225) and comments

so the in-network split, saves, sends and members reached are `null`. The tier model
on `wiki/audience.md` does not apply; the room is the owner's own following.

## Beat its cohort?

Yes, by a margin the lane has never seen. Scraped at 7.9 days, it sits in the
**1 to 4 weeks** experience cohort (n=12, median 205; n=11 and 200 without it) and
landed at 357x that median. The previous lane maximum was the 2025 Avatune launch at
1,271; this is 57x that. It is the lane maximum now, and it alone moved the lane's range in the report.

## Pillar and receipt

Pillar: **didnt-teach-me** — the build side is automated to 3-4 days from idea to MVP,
and marketing is where the deeper the owner goes, the less he delegates to AI. The
receipts are build numbers (fourth app, one day to bootstrap, three to polish, 3-4
days idea to MVP) and an ad outcome told without a dollar figure (zero results, a
couple of purchases, one more lifetime purchase). The cover was a real artifact: the
four apps' home screens side by side.

This is the third didnt-teach-me post and the third about the Meta-ads side of the
same operation. The first two (09-14 at 151, 09-28 at 172, both on rescrape) were ledgers: dollars in,
sign-ups out, every line a figure. This one is what the 09-28 retro asked for — what
the owner built and learned, with the ad results as one paragraph of support rather
than the spine. It is also filed mid-course ("Results are not clear yet"), which the
09-14 post was too, so mid-course filing does not sink a post on its own.

## The cause, and the decision

No validated anti-pattern applies, and the classifier reads it as a firsthand
experiment. The honest cause is room size, inverted: shipping apps with coding agents
in days is a subject a large share of technical builders have tried or want to, which
the Meta-ads ledgers and the process reports on [[experience]] were not. The turn
(the automation stops at marketing) gives it a second room, founders and marketers,
and the comment mix shows both arrived. One post is an outlier until the panel says
how far out of network it went, and the craft variables (artifact cover, owner-written,
non-ledger shape) all changed at once, so this cannot say which one carried it.

**Decision: repeat.** Keep the shape: lead with what the owner shipped, with the
build numbers, and turn on the part the automation could not do. Keep the money out
of the spine. Paste the panel for this post into the draft's `analytics:` block so the
