## The cause, and the decision

The craft was fine and the room was small. Two days earlier the 10-13 post put
shipping apps with agents in front of a large builder room; this one is a narrower
subject — one app's churn and the owner's design gap — with no shipped thing, no
screenshot, and a plan rather than a result. Whatever the 73,129 post reached, it did
not show up here: the follow-up landed exactly at its cohort median. Three variables
differ from the breakout (subject, artifact, a shipped thing in the body), so this does
not say which one mattered.

**Decision: modify.** Keep the user quotes as the opening; real users' words are a
better hook than the owner's ledger. Change two things for the next post on this app:
file it when the redesign exists, with the before-and-after screens as the cover
through `post-image`, and lead with what was shipped, as the 10-13 post did, with the
churn numbers as support.
